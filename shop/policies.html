Shipping, Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last updated:

1. Delivery Areas & Timing

We currently deliver across Hyderabad, with dedicated coverage in Kukatpally, Madhapur, and Gachibowli, and to other addresses within our service area as agreed with your account manager. Delivery slots and lead times are shown at checkout and are estimates, not guarantees — fresh produce sourcing and traffic conditions can occasionally affect timing.

2. Order Cancellation

You may cancel an order free of charge any time before it is marked "Confirmed" in your order tracking. Once an order has moved to "Packed" or later, it can no longer be cancelled through the Platform, since produce has already been picked for you — please contact us directly and we will do our best to accommodate reasonable requests.

3. Quality Issues & Returns

Because we deal in fresh produce, we do not accept general "change of mind" returns. If any item arrives damaged, spoiled, short in quantity, or materially different from what you ordered, please report it within 24 hours of delivery through your order page or by contacting us directly, ideally with a photo. We will offer, at our discretion, a replacement on your next order, a partial credit, or a refund for the affected item(s).

4. Refunds

Approved refunds for online payments are issued to the original payment method via our payment gateway and typically reflect within 5–7 business days, depending on your bank. For cash-on-delivery / invoiced accounts, an approved refund is applied as a credit against your next invoice unless you request otherwise.

5. Failed or Cancelled Payments

If an online payment fails or is not completed, your order is held as "Pending" and no amount is charged. You can retry payment from "My Orders", or switch to cash-on-delivery where available, before the order is confirmed.

6. Delivery Charges

Any applicable delivery fee is shown at checkout before you place your order. We may waive delivery charges for contracted business accounts as agreed separately.

7. Contact

For delivery issues, cancellations, or refund requests, please reach us via the contact details on your order confirmation or through your account manager.
