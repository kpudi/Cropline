Privacy Policy

Last updated:

This Privacy Policy explains how Cropline ("we", "us") collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you use our website and ordering platform ("Platform").

1. Information We Collect

  • Account details you provide: name, business name, phone number, email address, and password.
  • Delivery addresses you save to your address book.
  • Order details: items ordered, quantities, prices, order history, and any notes you add.
  • Payment confirmation data from our payment gateway (we do not receive or store your full card, UPI, or bank account details — these are handled directly by the gateway).
  • Communications you send us, including WhatsApp order messages, which we may use to create or update an order on your behalf.
  • Basic technical data such as browser type and pages visited, used to keep the Platform working correctly and securely.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account, and to fulfil, deliver, and invoice your orders.
  • To send you order status updates by email and/or WhatsApp (order confirmation, packed, out for delivery, delivered, or cancelled).
  • To provide customer support and respond to your queries.
  • To maintain accurate business records, including for tax and accounting purposes.
  • To improve the Platform and our catalog/pricing based on aggregate ordering trends.

3. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with:

  • Our payment gateway provider, to process online payments.
  • Our email and WhatsApp messaging providers, solely to deliver order-status notifications to you.
  • Our delivery personnel, limited to what is needed to complete your delivery (name, address, phone, order contents).
  • Law enforcement or regulators, where required by law.

4. Data Retention

We retain account and order information for as long as your account is active and thereafter as needed to meet accounting, tax, and legal obligations.

5. Your Rights

You may review and update your account details and address book at any time from "My Account". You may request deletion of your account by contacting us; we may retain order records where required by law (e.g., for tax purposes) even after account deletion.

6. Security

We use industry-standard measures, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) and access controls on our database, to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work to protect your data using suitable safeguards.

7. Cookies & Local Storage

We use your browser's local storage to remember items in your shopping cart before checkout. This data stays on your device and is not shared with third parties.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time; the "Last updated" date above will reflect the latest revision.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, please contact us via the details listed in your account or on your order confirmations.
